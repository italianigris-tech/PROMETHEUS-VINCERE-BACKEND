## Training Safety Decisions

Train now only after extraction support:

- number animation/no-animation by repeated mention
- CTA repetition count and low-cost variation
- semantic B-roll exactness
- graph explainer presence
- line-connected storyboard/list asset
- grouped miniaturize/blur focus transition
- text-as-object / nested text group

Manual-only for now:

- "glassmorphism-like" blur quality until visual examples are named.
- whether a line has true lens-flare treatment or just glow.
- mirror-surface polish value.
- whether a specific number had higher semantic punch than another.
- whether purple coherence is brand/theme or just video LUT.

Defer:

- exact motion/ease curve names.
- exact audio timing relations.
- shader/blur primitive taxonomy.
- automatic background color-sampling text until renderer support is designed.

Reject as direct training features:

- "Always animate the largest dollar number."
- "Always add SFX to text on talking head."
- "Every CTA should keep glow/gradient."
- "Every momentum chain must continue."
- "Every list must use numbers."
